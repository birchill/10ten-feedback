# 10ten feedback

This is where you can report bugs, suggest ideas, and ask questions about the [10ten app](https://app.10ten.life).

**[Go to Discussions →](https://github.com/birchill/10ten-feedback/discussions)**

Anyone can read the discussions. To post, comment, or upvote, you need a free GitHub account.

No account, or don't want to create one? Email us at [support@10ten.life](mailto:support@10ten.life) instead.

## Where to post

| You want to…                            | Post in                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Report something that's broken or wrong | [🐛 Bugs](https://github.com/birchill/10ten-feedback/discussions/new?category=bugs)   |
| Suggest a new feature or an improvement | [💡 Ideas](https://github.com/birchill/10ten-feedback/discussions/new?category=ideas) |
| Ask how something works or get help    | [🙋 Q&A](https://github.com/birchill/10ten-feedback/discussions/new?category=q-a)     |

Before you post, [search](https://github.com/birchill/10ten-feedback/discussions) to see if someone has already raised it. If they have, click the ▲ upvote arrow on their post or add a comment instead of starting a new one. Upvotes help us see what matters most.

You can write in English or Japanese.

## Reporting a bug

The more we know, the faster we can fix it. Tell us what you can:

- What you did, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead
- Your browser and device (for example, "Chrome on Windows" or "Safari on iPhone")
- The word or sentence involved, if it's about a search result, reading, or definition
- A screenshot, if you can

## What happens after you post

We read every post. We add labels to show where things stand, for example **planned**, **shipped**, or **not planned**. We can't always reply to everything, but your feedback helps us decide what to work on.

## Private or account issues

Everything here is public. Don't post your email address, account details, or anything else you'd rather keep private.

For problems with your account or anything personal, email us at [support@10ten.life](mailto:support@10ten.life).

## Using the 10ten Japanese Reader browser extension?

The extension has its own home: [birchill/10ten-ja-reader](https://github.com/birchill/10ten-ja-reader). Please post feedback about the extension there.
